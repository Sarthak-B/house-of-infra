How it works

Four steps from first visit to handover.

Step 1

Site visit & brief

We see the space, take measurements and hear how you want it to look and work.

Step 2

Design & estimate

You get the layout, materials and a clear estimate to approve before any work starts.

Step 3

Build & fit-out

Our team carries out the construction and interior work on site and keeps you updated.

Step 4

Finish & hand over

Lighting, furniture and final touches go in, and the space is handed over ready to use.
